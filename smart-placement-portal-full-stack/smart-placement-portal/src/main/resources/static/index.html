How It Works

From registration to placement, everything is managed seamlessly in one portal.

01
Register & Login
Students register with their academic details including branch, CGPA, and skills. Access the portal with secure credentials.
02

03
Apply Instantly
One-click application for eligible drives. The system automatically checks your CGPA, branch, and deadline before submitting.
04
Get Placed
Track your application status from Applied → Shortlisted → Interview → Selected. View your placement result in history.

Features

Everything You Need

Built for students and placement officers — a complete solution for modern campus recruitment.

Smart Eligibility Check
Automatic CGPA and branch eligibility verification before allowing any application. No confusion, no wasted time.
Real-Time Status Updates
Application statuses update instantly. Students see every change from Applied to Selected the moment the officer acts.
Placement Analytics
Placement cell gets complete analytics — total applications, shortlists, interviews, selections, and branch-wise statistics.
Company Management
Add, edit, and delete company profiles. Associate companies with placement drives and manage all information in one place.
Student Profiles
Students maintain complete profiles with academic info, skills, and resume. Editable at any time from the dashboard.
Secure Access Control
Separate portals for students and placement officers. Each user sees only what is relevant to their role.
